# KYC policy

## Why we do KYC
Regulations require us to verify every customer before they invest.

## When a KYC is rejected
A KYC submission is rejected when:
- the name on the PAN card does not match the name on the account,
- the identity document has expired, or
- the customer is younger than 18.

## Attempts
Customers can resubmit after a rejection. After **3 rejected attempts** the account is
**blocked** and only the support team can unblock it, after a manual review.

## Statuses
PENDING → APPROVED, or PENDING → REJECTED → (resubmit) → … → BLOCKED.
