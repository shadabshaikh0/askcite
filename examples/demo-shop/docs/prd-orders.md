# Orders PRD

Owner: Product · Status: Live

## Goal
Let verified customers invest in bonds and fixed deposits in a few taps, and change their mind
before the investment is settled.

## Placing an order
- Only customers whose **KYC is approved** can place orders.
- The minimum order is **₹1,000**.
- The product must be open for investment (status ACTIVE).

## Paying for an order
- Customers pay by UPI, net banking or card.
- If a payment fails, the customer can try again. **A customer gets 3 attempts in total**; after
  that the order is marked failed and the customer is told to place a new order.
- If money was debited on a failed attempt, it is refunded automatically.

## Cancelling an order
- Customers can cancel any order **before it is settled**.
- Unpaid orders are cancelled at once. Paid orders are refunded to the original payment method
  within **5 working days**.
- Settled orders cannot be cancelled; the customer can sell instead.

## Notifications
- Customers get a push notification when an order is paid, settled, cancelled or failed.
